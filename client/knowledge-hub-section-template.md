## Three things worth repeating

**Write it in your own words, always.** Not reworded from another POSH website. Near-duplicate content
does not rank and it is a copyright exposure for you. If a section is hard to write from scratch, that is
a good reason to record a voice note instead — not a reason to borrow.

**Do not stretch to fill the list.** If a section does not warrant 300 words, write 150 and tell us. If two
sections are really one, merge them. If something on the list is not a real question your clients ask, cut
it. Fewer sections written well is the better outcome, every time.

**Send them as they are finished.** Do not wait until all 14 are done. Send them in ones and twos and we
will load them as they arrive.
